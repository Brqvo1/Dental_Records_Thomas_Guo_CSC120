2. Main Method

## 3. Record Family Information Method. Collect, validate, and store family dental information.

3.1 Declare local variables.
3.2 Ask for family size (1–6) and validate input.
3.3 Loop through family members and record their names, rejecting empty or duplicate names.
3.4 Ask for upper and lower teeth strings.
3.5 Convert teeth to uppercase and validate length (1–8) and types (I, B, M).
3.6 Store names, tooth counts, and teeth in their respective arrays.

## 4. Print Family Records Method: Display all family dental records.

4.1 Declare local variables.
4.2 Loop through each family member and display their name.
4.3 Loop through upper and lower teeth.
4.4 Display each tooth's position and type, numbering from 1.
4.5 Repeat until every family member's record has been displayed.

## 5. Extract Tooth Method: Select a tooth and change it to missing.*

5.1 Declare local variables.
5.2 Ask for family member, tooth layer (U/L), and tooth number, validating each input.
5.3 Check that the selected layer has an available tooth.
5.4 Reject missing teeth (M), asking for another tooth number.
5.5 Change the selected tooth to M in the 3D array.
5.6 Return to the main menu.

## 6. Report Root Canal Indices Method: Count tooth types and calculate the family's root canal indices.*

6.1 Declare all local variables.
6.2 Initialize I, B, and M counts to zero.
6.3 Loop through all family members' upper and lower teeth, counting the total I, B, and M teeth.
6.4 If I > 0, calculate two roots using the quadratic formula: x = (-B +/- sqrt(B^2 + 4*I*M)) / (2*I)
6.5 If I = 0, solve the linear equation when B > 0. Otherwise, handle no solution or infinitely many solutions.
6.6 Display the calculated roots to two decimal places, or an appropriate message.
